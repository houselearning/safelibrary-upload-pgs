Why was HouseLearning Pages Created?

HouseLearning Pages was created on the 26th of January, in some 2026 year. On this date, the creator, (me) felt like that HouseLearning was missing something - a pages website specifically designed for learners trying to learn the HTML language. Fast foward a little less then 24 hours, and here we are, on the 27th of January now, with exactly 1 user using this tool. Me! (depressing, really.). Alex took time out of his Monday to spend countless hours on his computer, trying to deploy this site. And now, he did. But each website could hold exactly 1MB of storage due to Firebase's file storage rules on the free plan. First, it was run locally on Alex's computer, then published, now one person uses it! Huge milestone. But yes, this is how and why the HouseLearning pages were created.
